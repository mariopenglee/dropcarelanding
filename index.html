How it works

From surgery to recovery — covered.

Your clinic sets up your drop schedule. You just follow the reminders.

1

Your clinic sets it up

Your surgeon creates your personalised drop schedule in the Dropcare admin system before you leave.

2

You get an activation code

A short code sent to your phone or email. Enter it in the app — your schedule loads instantly.

3

Follow your reminders

Dropcare notifies you at every dose time. Tap to confirm, and your clinic can see your progress.

Features

Built for recovery.

Everything you need to stay on track — nothing you don't.

⏰

Dose alarms

Notifications fire at every scheduled dose time, so you never need to guess when your next drop is due.

📋

Full schedule view
